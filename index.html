And then the silence came.

But love, true love, does not end with silence. It waits. It hopes. It endures.

He: What shall I tell God? That I have fallen in the hands of an Asian girl, bright as the rising of the sun.

She: For the black color is like a beautiful flower under the morning sunshine; I delight when I look at it.

He: Do not go, oh Asian girl; for I will miss your speech.

She: Wait until you get into my arms; you will like sleeping like you have never before.

He: I fear to lose you.

She: I fear to lose you too, maybe someday, but not now.

He: I love you so much, my love. You should know.

She: I miss you so badly.

He: Hi, love.

She was Nazurah. He was Leon. And their story, told between two worlds across Telegram and WhatsApp, became something sacred — a testament to the truth that love finds a way, even when every force conspires against it.

For love is patient. Love is kind. Love does not envy or boast. It is not proud. It does not dishonor others, it is not self-seeking, it is not easily angered, it keeps no record of wrongs.

And love, across oceans and time zones, across outages and elections, across fear and distance and the silence of severed networks, will find its voice again.

Always yours.

Fin.

Based on real conversations between Leon and Nazurah.

Telegram and WhatsApp, December 16, 2025 – January 21, 2026.
