# 💕 The Dialogues with My Love

**A novel based on a true story.**

*By Leon & Nazurah*

---

This is the story of two souls who found each other across continents. She, an Asian girl from the far East. He, an African boy from Uganda. What began as a simple greeting on Telegram became something neither expected — a love story written in messages, voice notes, and the quiet hours between time zones.

This book is a testimony that love does not ask for proximity; it asks for truth. Through conversations, arguments, prayers, laughter, and tears, "The Dialogues with My Love" captures the intimate journey of two young hearts learning to belong to each other.
